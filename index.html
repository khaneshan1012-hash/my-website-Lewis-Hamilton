Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international auto racing for single-seater formula cars. Governed by the FIA, it features top global teams and drivers competing on circuits worldwide for the World Championships.

Formula One (F1) is the highest level of international open-wheel, single-seater motor racing, sanctioned by the FIA. The first F1 World Championship was held in 1950. A season consists of races called Grands Prix, held around the world on specially built or street circuits. Drivers and teams compete for the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championships using a points system. F1 cars are among the fastest racing cars because of their advanced aerodynamics, downforce, engines, suspension, and tyres. Since 2017, F1 has been owned commercially by Liberty Media. The 2026 cost cap is US$215 million, although teams can spend more on areas outside the cap. The United Kingdom is a major F1 hub, with six of the eleven teams based there.

Sir Lewis Hamilton

Sir Lewis Hamilton is a British racing driver for Ferrari and a seven-time Formula One World Champion, tied with Michael Schumacher. Known as one of the greatest drivers in the history of the sport, he holds record numbers for career race wins (106) and pole positions (104), having raced for McLaren, Mercedes, and Ferrari

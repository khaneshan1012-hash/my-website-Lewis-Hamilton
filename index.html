Inside & Outside F1 - Lewis Hamilton Edition

Formula 1

Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international auto racing for single-seater formula cars. Governed by the FIA, it features top global teams and drivers competing on circuits around the world for the Drivers' and Constructors' World Championships.

The first Formula One World Championship was held in 1950. A Formula 1 season consists of races called Grands Prix, which take place at specially built racing circuits and street circuits around the world.

Formula 1 cars are designed around advanced aerodynamics, downforce, suspension, tyres and powerful hybrid power units. Drivers must combine speed, precision, race strategy and tyre management to compete successfully over a Grand Prix weekend.

Formula 1 has developed significantly since its first championship. The sport has introduced different generations of cars, engines and technical regulations while continuing to bring together some of the world's most talented drivers and engineers.

Sir Lewis Hamilton

Sir Lewis Carl Davidson Hamilton is a British Formula 1 racing driver. He was born on 7 January 1985 in Stevenage, England, and made his Formula 1 debut with McLaren in 2007.

Hamilton quickly established himself in Formula 1. During his debut season he achieved multiple podium finishes and competed for the World Championship. In 2008, he won his first Drivers' World Championship with McLaren.

Hamilton later moved to Mercedes for the 2013 season. His years with Mercedes became a major part of his career, during which he won six more World Championships.

Hamilton joined Ferrari for the 2025 Formula 1 season, beginning another chapter of his career with one of the most famous teams in motorsport. Ferrari's 2026 driver line-up includes Hamilton alongside Charles Leclerc.


Lewis Hamilton's Formula 1 Career

Lewis Hamilton began his Formula 1 career with McLaren in 2007. His first season was highly successful for a rookie, and he finished second in the Drivers' Championship.

In 2008, Hamilton won his first Formula 1 World Championship. He became the youngest World Champion at the time, although that record has since been surpassed.

Hamilton moved from McLaren to Mercedes for the 2013 season. His first Mercedes victory came at the 2013 Hungarian Grand Prix. The following year, he won his second World Championship during the beginning of Mercedes' highly successful turbo-hybrid era.

Between 2014 and 2020, Hamilton won six World Championships. He became a consistent front-runner during this period and achieved a large number of victories, podiums and pole positions.

Hamilton moved to Ferrari for the 2025 season. During the 2026 season he continued racing for Ferrari and achieved his first Grand Prix victory with the team at the Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix.

Lewis Hamilton's Achievements

Hamilton has won seven Formula One World Championships. His championships came in 2008, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020.

His career statistics include 106 Grand Prix victories, 207 podium finishes and 104 pole positions. He has entered 395 Grands Prix and has scored 5,217.5 career points.

Lewis Hamilton's F1 Teams

Throughout his Formula 1 career, Lewis Hamilton has raced for three teams: McLaren, Mercedes and Ferrari.

🔴 McLaren

McLaren gave Hamilton his Formula 1 debut in 2007. He spent his first six seasons in F1 with the team and won his first World Championship with McLaren in 2008.

⚫ Mercedes

Hamilton joined Mercedes in 2013. The partnership became one of the most successful periods of his career. He won six World Championships with Mercedes and achieved many of his career victories and pole positions with the team.

🔴 Ferrari

Hamilton joined Ferrari for the 2025 season. Ferrari is one of the longest-running and most recognisable teams in Formula 1. Hamilton continues to race for Ferrari during the 2026 season.

Lewis Hamilton's Records and Statistics

Hamilton has accumulated statistics across nearly two decades of Formula 1 competition. His career totals include 106 race victories, 104 pole positions and 207 podium finishes.

🏁 Grand Prix Wins

Hamilton has won 106 Formula 1 Grands Prix. His victories have come at circuits around the world and across several different generations of Formula 1 cars.

🎯 Pole Positions

Hamilton has taken 104 pole positions. Pole position is awarded to the driver who records the fastest qualifying time for the starting grid.

🏅 Podium Finishes

Hamilton has achieved 207 podium finishes. A podium finish means finishing the race in first, second or third position.

🏆 Championships

Hamilton has won seven Drivers' World Championships, matching the championship total achieved by Michael Schumacher.

Memorable Moments in Lewis Hamilton's Career

🇧🇷 2008 Brazilian Grand Prix

The 2008 Brazilian Grand Prix decided the Drivers' Championship. Hamilton secured his first World Championship at the final race of the season.

🇬🇧 British Grand Prix Victories

The British Grand Prix at Silverstone has been one of Hamilton's strongest circuits. He has won the race nine times, more than any other driver at the event.

🇧🇪 2024 Belgian Grand Prix

Hamilton won the Belgian Grand Prix in 2024 during his final season with Mercedes.

🇬🇧 2024 British Grand Prix

Hamilton won the 2024 British Grand Prix at Silverstone, producing an emotional home victory during his final seasons with Mercedes.

🇪🇸 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix

Hamilton secured his first Grand Prix victory for Ferrari at the 2026 Barcelona-Catalunya Grand Prix. He finished ahead of the other drivers to take Ferrari to victory at the circuit.

Lewis Hamilton's Driving Style

Formula 1 drivers need to manage many different factors during a race. These include tyre performance, fuel and energy management, braking, cornering, overtaking and changing weather conditions.

Hamilton has competed through several different generations of Formula 1 cars. His career has included the V8 engine era, the turbo-hybrid era and the new regulations introduced for the 2026 season.

Qualifying has also been an important part of Hamilton's career. His 104 pole positions demonstrate his success over a single qualifying lap.
